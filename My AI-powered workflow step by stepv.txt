STEP1. MARKET SNAPSHOT

Analyze BTCUSDT right now.

Give me:

* Current price and recent performance
* Volume and volatility
* Current trend
* Important support and resistance
* Major recent changes

Separate facts from interpretation.

---

STEP2. WHAT IS DRIVING BTC?

Identify the main things currently affecting BTCUSDT.

Look at:

* Recent news and events
* Major catalysts
* Macro conditions
* Important upcoming events

For each major driver, explain **what happened, why it matters, and what evidence supports it.**

---

STEP3. FUNDAMENTALS & ON-CHAIN

Investigate whether the data supports the current Bitcoin narrative.

Analyze the most relevant:

* Fundamental developments
* Network activity
* Exchange flows
* Whale activity
* Institutional flows
* Other important on-chain metrics

Tell me what the data **supports, contradicts, or cannot confirm**.

---

STEP4. SENTIMENT & TECHNICALS

Analyze both market sentiment and technical structure.

**Sentiment:**

* Main bullish arguments
* Main bearish arguments
* Which arguments have evidence?
* Which are speculation?

**Technicals:**

* 4H trend
* Daily trend
* Weekly trend
* Key support/resistance
* Important breakout/breakdown levels
* What would invalidate the current structure?

---

STEP5. CHALLENGE THE THESIS

Act as a skeptical analyst.

Look for contradictions across everything we researched.

Ask:

* What doesn't fit the dominant narrative?
* What evidence contradicts the bullish case?
* What evidence contradicts the bearish case?
* Are there important missing pieces?
* What assumptions are being made?
* What information could change our interpretation?

Give me the **strongest argument against the current interpretation**.

---

STEP6. BUILD SCENARIOS

Create three conditional scenarios for BTCUSDT:

**Bullish**

* What would need to happen?
* What evidence would confirm it?
* What would invalidate it?

**Neutral**

* What would need to happen?
* What evidence would confirm it?
* What would invalidate it?

**Bearish**

* What would need to happen?
* What evidence would confirm it?
* What would invalidate it?

Do not assign probabilities or predict which scenario will happen.

---

STEP7. FINAL DEEP-DIVE REPORT

Now combine everything into a concise report.

Use this structure:

# BTCUSDT MARKET DEEP DIVE

**Current Situation**
What is happening?

**Key Drivers**
Why is it happening?

**Fundamentals & On-Chain**
What does the data show?

**Sentiment**
What are bulls and bears saying, and what is supported by evidence?

**Technical Structure**
What does the chart show?

**Contradictions**
What doesn't fit the main narrative?

**Scenarios**
Bullish / Neutral / Bearish — with conditions and invalidation points.

**What to Watch**
List the most important events, data, levels and signals to monitor next.

**Bottom Line**
Summarize what we know, what we don't know, and what could change the current interpretation.

Do not make a simple "BTC will go up/down" prediction. Base the analysis on evidence and clearly distinguish facts, interpretations and uncertainties.
