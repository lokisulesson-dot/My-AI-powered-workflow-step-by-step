Step 1 — Define the research question

You are my market research assistant.

I want to conduct a deep dive into BTCUSDT.

First, define the exact research question we should investigate.

Identify:

1. The current market narrative surrounding BTCUSDT.
2. The major factors that could materially affect BTCUSDT.
3. The key uncertainties we need to investigate.
4. The specific questions we should answer before reaching any conclusion.

Do not predict the future price.
Do not assume the current narrative is correct.
Separate confirmed information from interpretation and speculation.

Output:

* Primary research question
* Current narratives
* Key factors
* Key uncertainties
* Research questions

Investigate the catalysts currently affecting BTCUSDT.

Analyze the macroeconomic environment surrounding BTCUSDT.

* Inflation

* US dollar strength
* Treasury yields

Step 9 — Challenge the thesis

Act as a skeptical research analyst.

Review everything we have established about BTCUSDT so far.

Your job is NOT to confirm the current narrative.

Your job is to try to break it.

Identify:

1. Contradictions between price and fundamentals.
2. Contradictions between technicals and on-chain data.
3. Contradictions between sentiment and measurable data.
4. Contradictions between macro conditions and the current BTC narrative.
5. Evidence that contradicts the dominant bullish argument.
6. Evidence that contradicts the dominant bearish argument.
7. Important information that has been ignored.
8. Claims that rely primarily on assumptions.
9. Data that could be misleading or taken out of context.
10. Evidence that would materially change our interpretation.

Then answer:

"What is the strongest argument against our current interpretation of BTCUSDT?"

Be deliberately skeptical.

Do not introduce unsupported claims merely to create disagreement.

Step 10 — Build the scenarios

Based only on the evidence collected so far, construct three possible BTCUSDT scenarios:

1. Bullish scenario
2. Neutral scenario
3. Bearish scenario

For each scenario provide:

* Scenario description
* Evidence supporting it
* Relevant catalysts
* Macro conditions required
* Fundamental conditions
* On-chain conditions
* Technical conditions
* Sentiment conditions
* What would confirm the scenario
* What would invalidate the scenario
* What data/events should be monitored

Do NOT assign probabilities.

Do NOT declare which scenario will happen.

Frame each scenario as a conditional pathway:

"If X, Y and Z occur, this scenario becomes more consistent with the available evidence."

Clearly distinguish evidence from assumptions.
